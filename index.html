Key. Dashed line: valley fold. Dash-dot-dot line: mountain fold. Thin solid line: existing crease. Arrows arc on the outside of the flap they move and are hidden by paper in front of them. Filled arrowhead: valley fold; hollow one-sided head: mountain fold; hollow head at the tail: unfold; broad hollow arrow: push (reverse fold). Valley and mountain are as seen from where you look: when the model turns over they swap.
